01
THE BEGINNING

How We Met

Some friendships begin with a simple introduction. Ours began years before we realized that three different paths would eventually lead to a bond this special💓.

Mariama — The Rival Who Made Me Better💕

I met Mariama Dansoko in primary school. She was a year ahead of me, and even then, I already knew her name. She was exceptionally smart and consistently ranked at the top of her class.

We both passed our national examinations and later found ourselves in the same class in JSS One. That was when our academic rivalry began. I had always heard about how smart she was, and I wanted to see if I could beat the girl everyone talked about.

JSS One gave me my answer: she came first. She beat me. But instead of discouraging me, that defeat challenged me to work harder. I wanted to do better, and our competition continued throughout our school years. Sometimes she won, and sometimes I did. We pushed each other to improve, becoming the smartest girl and the smartest boy in class.

After another national examination, we both passed and, almost unbelievably, attended the same senior secondary school. Although we were placed in different classes, our connection continued. What began as competition became an important part of our journey.

Isatu — From Greetings to a Special Bond💕

I met Isatu Kargbo later, at the same senior secondary school, around SSS Two. At first, we weren't particularly close. I knew her through mutual friends, especially Jenny Aisha, and our circles naturally overlapped.

We greeted each other and talked occasionally. Somewhere along the way, I started calling her "Auntie," while she called me "her son." It was a funny little tradition that became part of our story. Isatu was calm, lovely, and smart, someone you naturally enjoyed having around.

By SSS three, especially during the second term, our bond had grown much stronger. We became genuinely close, and after sitting for WAEC and leaving school, our friendship only grew deeper.

Different beginnings. Different stories. One beautiful friendship💕.

One friendship began with academic rivalry and the desire to become better. The other grew through mutual friends, simple greetings, and little moments that gradually became meaningful.

We didn't know it then, but those ordinary school days were quietly laying the foundation for something special. Sometimes, the most meaningful friendships begin without us even realizing it.

And this was only the beginning of our story💕.

02
GROWING CLOSER

How We Got Close

Not every friendship becomes special overnight. Sometimes, it grows quietly through shared experiences, little conversations, friendly competition, and the simple joy of having certain people around.

From Competition to Connection

For Mariama and me, our connection began in the classroom. What started as an academic rivalry gradually became something meaningful. We challenged each other, pushed each other to do better, and learned that having someone who inspires you can make the journey even more rewarding. Somewhere along the way, the competition became part of a story bigger than who came first.

From Simple Greetings to Something Deeper

With Isatu, things happened differently. We started with occasional greetings, mutual friends, and little conversations. Even our funny "Auntie" and "son" names became part of the memories we shared. At first, we weren't especially close, but as time passed, our connection grew.

By SSS Three, especially during the second term, we had become genuinely close. And when school ended after WAEC, our friendship didn't end with it. Instead, it grew even deeper, proving that some connections can continue to flourish long after the classroom days are over.

Somehow, our different paths began leading us toward the same kind of friendship💖.

One bond grew through competition and mutual respect. Another grew through familiarity, laughter, and time. Neither needed a grand beginning. They simply needed room to grow.

Looking back, perhaps becoming close was never about one big moment. It was about all those ordinary days that slowly became meaningful, until the people who once shared the same school environment became people whose friendship mattered far beyond school💖.

And little by little, our story became more than just how we met. It became about the bond we continued to build💕💖.

03
MORE THAN A COINCIDENCE

When It Became "Us"

Sometimes, you don't realize that something special is taking shape until you look back and connect the pieces. Our story wasn't built in a single day. It grew through different friendships, different experiences, and years of crossing paths.

Three Paths, One Connection

Mariama had been part of my journey since primary school. What began as an academic rivalry grew into a connection built on challenge, respect, and the desire to become better. We shared classrooms, competed with each other, and continued our journey into senior secondary school.

Isatu came into my life later, through mutual friends and simple conversations. At first, we were just familiar faces in the same school environment. But with time, our greetings became something more, and our friendship grew into a bond I came to value deeply.

Our beginnings were different, but both friendships became meaningful parts of my life. And as our journeys continued beyond school, I began to appreciate how special it was to have people whose presence brought something different and valuable into my world.

Maybe "us" was never about one particular day. Maybe it was about everything that led us here.

The classrooms, the competition, the greetings, the laughter, the familiar names, and the friendships that continued even after school had ended. Each part of the journey meant something, even when we didn't recognize it at the time.

We didn't need to have the same beginning to become part of the same story. Our paths were different, but each connection added something worth keeping.

And somewhere along the way, this stopped being just a story about how we met. It became a story about the people we found in one another and the friendship we continue to cherish.
